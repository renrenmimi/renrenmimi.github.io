Pet-Centric Social Platform

02

Real-Time AI Communication Coach

ToneDown

A real-time coach that scores how you're coming across as you speak — live transcription, LLM tone analysis, and audio signals combined into one score. It adds roleplay "sparring" personas, AI session recaps, and a zero-mic demo, all served through a Vercel serverless proxy to Groq (Whisper + Llama 3.3 70B) with a circuit-breaker fallback chain.

03

Immigration Timeline Tracker

GreenLane

A server-rendered dashboard for immigration backlogs: US green card categories (EB-1 through EB-5, NIW, family-based) alongside Canada's Express Entry draws. Ten years of history, per-category wait estimates, and email alerts when a cutoff date moves — fed by three scheduled pipelines that pull from official sources only.
